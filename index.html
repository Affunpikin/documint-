GOOD TO KNOW

Quick answers.

Is DocuMint free to use?＋

The current starter version lets you create, edit, save drafts in this browser, and print or save documents as PDFs for free. Paid plans are not enabled yet.

Do I need to create an account?＋

No account is needed for the current version. Drafts are saved in your browser on this device, so they won't automatically appear on another device.

How do I download a PDF?＋

Choose “Print / Save PDF” and select “Save as PDF” in your browser's print window. On mobile, choose the PDF or print option available in your system share/print menu.

Can I use it outside Nigeria?＋

Yes. You can choose from Nigerian naira, US dollars, British pounds, euros, Ghanaian cedis, Kenyan shillings, and South African rand.
